# References and External Ontologies

HNEO is designed to interoperate selectively with established semantic standards.

## Core semantic references

- CIDOC Conceptual Reference Model (CIDOC CRM): event-centered cultural heritage and historical semantics.
- CRMinf: argumentation, propositions, beliefs, inference, and evidential reasoning.
- W3C PROV-O: provenance.
- W3C Organization Ontology (ORG): organizations, posts, memberships, organizational structure.
- OWL-Time: temporal intervals and ordering.
- FIBO: financial and business concepts.
- SKOS: controlled vocabularies.
- SHACL: graph validation.
- OntoUML/UFO: conceptual design discipline, especially roles, identity, and relators.
- Nanopublications: source-bounded publication of atomic assertions.

## Historical source families represented by the example

The worked dataset is synthetic and should eventually be replaced or supplemented with sourced claims derived from:
- U.S. congressional investigations;
- declassified intelligence records;
- court records;
- independent-counsel reports;
- archival collections;
- scholarly secondary sources;
- carefully attributed investigative journalism and memoir.

The schema is intentionally capable of storing both documented findings and disputed allegations without collapsing them into a single truth layer.
