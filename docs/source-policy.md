# Source Policy

HNEO is source-centered.

## Preferred hierarchy

1. archival primary records
2. court records
3. congressional or commission records
4. inspector-general or independent-counsel reports
5. declassified intelligence records
6. contemporaneous reporting
7. scholarly secondary literature
8. investigative books and journalism
9. memoir or retrospective testimony
10. unsourced or anonymous allegations

This ordering is not an automatic reliability ranking. A primary source can contain falsehoods and a secondary source can correct them.

## Required source granularity

Where possible, claims should cite a source fragment rather than merely a whole book or report.

Store document identifier, page, section, paragraph, archival collection, URI, and quotation span where permitted.

## Allegations

Allegations are retained when historically important but must remain explicitly attributed and must not be promoted into world assertions without evidence.
